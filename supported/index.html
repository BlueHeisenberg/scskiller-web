Supported engines

What SCSKiller 1.3.0 reads
EngineCoversSince

  1. Unreal Engine 4

    Covers: DirectX 11 and 12, like Returnal, Deep Rock Galactic and Ghostrunner. Before 4.20, DirectX 12 games need a short recordingSince: since 1.0.0

  2. Unreal Engine 5

    Covers: Up to 5.8, DirectX 11 and 12, like The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion Remastered, SILENT HILL f and SILENT HILL: TownfallSince: since 1.0.0

  3. Unreal Engine 4 and 5 with an AES key

    Covers: Encrypted games, the key is read from the game's exe or you paste it, like DRAGON BALL: Sparking! ZERO and Dead by DaylightSince: since 1.0.0

  4. Modified Unreal Engine 4 (per-game support)

    Covers: Studio versions with their own rules, FINAL FANTASY VII REBIRTH, Hogwarts Legacy and STAR WARS Jedi: Survivor, DirectX 12Since: since 1.0.0

  5. Unreal Engine 4 in Wuthering Waves

    Covers: Wuthering Waves, DirectX 12, needs its AES key. No recording on NVIDIA. On AMD it needs a recording, and the game contains anti-cheat (enabling the recorder there is at your own risk)Since: since 1.3.0-beta.2

  6. Unity

    Covers: DirectX 11 and 12, like Megabonk. DirectX 12 needs a recordingSince: since 1.0.0

  7. RE Engine

    Covers: DirectX 11 and 12, like Monster Hunter Wilds and Resident Evil 2. On DirectX 12 only PRAGMATA and Resident Evil Village compile without a recording, on NVIDIASince: since 1.0.0

  8. Dagor Engine

    Covers: War Thunder, DirectX 11 and 12Since: since 1.2.1

  9. Northlight

    Covers: Control on DirectX 11 and 12, Alan Wake 2 on DirectX 12. Alan Wake 2 and CONTROL Resonant need a recordingSince: since 1.2.1

  10. REDengine 3

    Covers: The Witcher 3, DirectX 12Since: since 1.1.1

  11. REDengine 4

    Covers: Cyberpunk 2077, DirectX 12, needs a recordingSince: since 1.0.0

  12. Decima

    Covers: Horizon Zero Dawn Complete Edition and Death Stranding Director's Cut, DirectX 12, needs a recordingSince: since 1.3.0-alpha.1

  13. RAGE

    Covers: Grand Theft Auto V Enhanced, DirectX 12, no recording on NVIDIASince: since 1.3.0-alpha.1

  14. ForzaTech

    Covers: Forza Horizon 6, DirectX 12. Ray tracing needs a recording, and the game contains anti-cheat (enabling the recorder there is at your own risk)Since: since 1.3.0-alpha.2

  15. Source 2

    Covers: Counter-Strike 2, Deadlock and Dota 2, DirectX 11Since: since 1.3.0-alpha.1

  16. Luminous Engine

    Covers: Final Fantasy XV Windows Edition, DirectX 11Since: since 1.3.0-alpha.2

  17. FromSoftware's engine

    Covers: Elden Ring and Elden Ring Nightreign on DirectX 12, Dark Souls III and Dark Souls: Remastered on DirectX 11Since: since 1.0.0

  18. Naughty Dog's engine

    Covers: The Last of Us Part I and UNCHARTED: Legacy of Thieves Collection, DirectX 12, needs a recordingSince: since 1.3.0-alpha.1

  19. Santa Monica Studio's engine

    Covers: God of War on DirectX 11, God of War Ragnarök on DirectX 12Since: since 1.3.0-alpha.1

  20. Insomniac Engine

    Covers: Ratchet & Clank: Rift Apart, DirectX 12. No recording on NVIDIA for the Steam build SCSKiller's rules cover, other builds need oneSince: since 1.3.0-alpha.1

  21. Sucker Punch's engine

    Covers: Ghost of Tsushima, DirectX 12. No recording on NVIDIA for the Steam build SCSKiller's rules cover, other builds need oneSince: since 1.3.0-alpha.1

  22. Stingray

    Covers: HELLDIVERS 2, DirectX 12, needs a recording. The game contains anti-cheat (enabling the recorder there is at your own risk)Since: since 1.3.0-alpha.1

  23. Overwatch's engine

    Covers: Overwatch on Steam, DirectX 11Since: since 1.3.0-alpha.1

  24. Other engines

    Covers: Games that ship ready DirectX shaders, like Starfield and Marvel's Guardians of the GalaxySince: since 1.0.0
