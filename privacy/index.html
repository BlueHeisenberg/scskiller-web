Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

In short

  • SCSKiller runs on your PC. Everything it does there works offline and without an account.
  • Your data leaves your PC only when you sign in with Patreon, share recordings (opt-in), download from the community database, or update.
  • We never receive your name or email address. Shared recordings are anonymous and never linked to your account.
  • This website has no cookies, analytics or trackers.

Who is responsible

SCSKiller is the data controller. Contact: contact@scskiller.com. See the legal notice.

When the app goes online

Welcome messageOn first start, the app downloads its welcome text from our server. The request carries no account or device id.
Stable updatesCome from GitHub Releases. GitHub's privacy statement applies.
Beta and alpha buildsCome from our server, for signed-in supporters.
Game format dataFor some games, the app downloads public files from GitHub that it needs to read the game's archives. No data about you is sent.
Sign-in, database, uploadsDescribed below.

Sign-in with Patreon

Patreon tells us your Patreon user id and your membership of the SCSKiller campaign. We don't request your name, email address or other memberships.

We store:

  • your Patreon user id and member id;
  • your tier, membership status, paid-through date, last charge status and when we last checked them;
  • a random account reference, the only account id used outside our database;
  • your signed-in devices: a random id, a label, when each was added and last used. Device tokens are stored hashed.

We don't store your name, email address, picture, postal address, payment amounts or Patreon login tokens. Your IP address is not stored with your account.

Sharing recordings (opt-in)

Sharing is off by default. Before the first upload, the app shows you exactly what would be sent.

An upload contains shader hashes and how the game combines them into pipelines (root signatures, formats and other pipeline state). It contains no shader code. It also reveals:

  • that this device has the game and which build (store id, build id, content hash);
  • your GPU vendor;
  • roughly which parts of the game you played;
  • the names of middleware DLLs next to the game, which can reveal mods such as upscaler mods.

Not sent: your driver version, file paths, the game's exe name or your Windows user name.

Uploads use a random id for this install, which you can reset. They are never linked to your Patreon account, even when you are signed in.

Server logs

  • Connection logs keep a truncated IP address (the last part removed) for 7 days, for security and rate limiting.
  • A security log records sign-ins, device changes and membership changes, by account reference, for 90 days.

Legal bases

Account, membership, devicesPerforming our contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
Truncated IP logs, security logOur legitimate interest in keeping the service secure (Art. 6(1)(f)).
UploadsYour opt-in, and our legitimate interest in running the community database (Art. 6(1)(f)).

No special categories of data. No profiling.

Who else processes data

CloudflareProcessor. Carries traffic to our server, so it processes IP addresses and requests. Based in the US: transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
Hosting providerProcessor. Hosts our server in the EU (Spain).
Backblaze B2Processor. Stores encrypted backups only.
PatreonIndependent controller for your Patreon account and payments. Patreon's privacy policy applies.
GitHubIndependent controller. Hosts this website and the stable downloads, and sees visitors' IP addresses.

Retention

  • Account data: while you have a membership. Former supporters' data is deleted 12 months after their last membership ends.
  • Connection logs: 7 days. Security log: 90 days.
  • Backups: rotate out within 30 days.
  • Shared recordings: kept as part of the database. They are anonymous.

Your rights

You can access, delete, correct or port your data, object to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdraw consent at any time. In the app, your account settings let you download your data or delete your account. Deleting it removes your Patreon link, membership and devices. Or write to contact@scskiller.com.

You can also complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the AEPD.

This website

No cookies, analytics, trackers or third-party requests. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub processes your IP address to serve it.
